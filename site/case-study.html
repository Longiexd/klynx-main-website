From WhatsApp chaos
to a fully automated office

Here's what we built for a visa agency — and what changed for their team.

The client

Visa agency

Tunis, Tunisia · Immigration & visa services · Team of 8

0 Missed appointments since go-live
0 Lost client documents after migration
100% Team migrated off WhatsApp & spreadsheets
↑ Lead conversion rate — no more leads lost to disorganization

Before Klynx

The team managed every client application through WhatsApp groups and multiple spreadsheets. Appointments were missed. Document checklists were sent manually per client. Leads went cold because no one followed up. The director had zero visibility on what was happening in the office in real time.

What we built

We hosted Odoo on a dedicated server and migrated the entire team onto one platform. We configured a custom CRM pipeline for each application stage, automated document checklists sent to clients on intake, set up appointment scheduling, and built a live manager dashboard tracking invoices, active cases, and employee productivity. We added WikiJS for internal documentation with automated suggestions — so the team stopped losing time asking each other basic questions. Automated emails and rescheduling suggestions for unclosed leads meant no opportunity was forgotten.

Odoo Custom CRM Appointment scheduling Document automation WikiJS Manager dashboard Lead automation Dedicated server Linux server administration

The result

The team became efficient within weeks. No more lost leads from disorganization. No more forgotten appointments or missing client documents. Managers now have a live view of everything happening in the office. The business went from reactive to structured — and is now built to scale.
